## KLUSTER 1: GenAI, Integritas Akademik, & Keterbatasan Deteksi AI Black-Box (16 Paper)
*Fokus: Mengapa detektor plagiasi komersial gagal, bias pada penulis non-native, dan perlunya paradigma formatif.*

1. **Perkins, M., Roe, J., Vu, B. H., Postma, D., & Hickerson, D. (2024).**  
   *GenAI detection tools' vulnerabilities: A cross-institutional examination of accuracy and student perception.*  
   *Computers & Education*, 212, 104998.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.compedu.2024.104998](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.104998)  
   *Anotasi*: Mengungkap false-positive rate detektor AI komersial yang mencapai >14% pada tulisan mahasiswa, menuntut evaluasi proses daripada deteksi teks statis.

5. **Weber-Wulff, D., Anohina-Naumeca, A., Bjelobaba, S., et al. (2023).**  
   *Testing of detection tools for AI-generated text.*

21. **Kalyuga, S., & Singh, A. M. (2021).**  
    *Rethinking the expertise reversal effect in adaptive learning environments.*

    DOI: [10.1007/s10648-020-09579-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09579-2)

22. **Skulmowski, A., & Rey, G. D. (2020).**  
    *COVID-19 as an accelerator for computer-based testing: A cognitive load perspective on exam stress and time pressures.*  
    *Frontiers in Psychology*, 11, 2448.  
    DOI: [10.3389/fpsyg.2020.02448](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.02448)

23. **Chong, S. W., & Reinders, H. (2022).**  
    *Technology-mediated task-based language teaching: A cognitive load perspective.*  
    *Language Learning & Technology*, 26(1), 1-18.  
    DOI: [10.125/73467](https://doi.org/10.125/73467)  
    *Anotasi*: Memberikan landasan mengapa aktivasi berbasis ketukan pertama (*first-keystroke activation*) memberi ruang priming kognitif yang menenangkan mahasiswa.

24. **Guzman, E., Conejo, R., & Perez-de-la-Cruz, J. L. (2021).**  
    *Adaptive testing based on cognitive state tracking and time-to-first-action metrics.*  
    *IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies*, 14(2), 215-227.

25. **De Smet, C., De Wever, B., Schellens, T., & Valcke, M. (2022).**

26. **Zeidner, M. (2021).**

    *Educational Psychologist*, 56(4), 260-278.  
    DOI: [10.1080/00461520.2021.1968875](https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2021.1968875)

27. **Leppink, J., Paas, F., Van der Vleuten, C. P., et al. (2023).**
